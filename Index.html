Grapes

grapes are small, round, and juicy fruits that grow in clusters on vines. They come in various colors, including green, red, and purple, and are enjoyed fresh or used to make wine, juice, and raisins.

kiwi

kiwi is a small, brown, fuzzy fruit with bright green flesh and tiny black seeds. It has a sweet and tangy flavor and is rich in vitamin C, making it a healthy and refreshing snack.

Manggo

Manggo is a tropical fruit known for its sweet and juicy flesh. It has a vibrant orange color and is often enjoyed fresh, in smoothies, or as a topping for desserts.

Strawberry

strawberries are bright red, juicy fruits with a sweet flavor and tiny seeds on their surface. They are commonly eaten fresh, used in desserts, or made into jams and preserves.

pinya

pinya, also known as pineapple, is a tropical fruit with a spiky exterior and sweet, tangy flesh. It is often enjoyed fresh, juiced, or used in cooking and desserts.

rambutan

rambutan is a tropical fruit with a hairy red or yellow skin and juicy, translucent flesh. It has a sweet and slightly acidic taste and is often eaten fresh or used in desserts.

Watermelon

Watermelon is a refreshing summer fruit with a juicy, sweet flesh and a green rind. It is often enjoyed fresh, in salads, or as a hydrating snack.
